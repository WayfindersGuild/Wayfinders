The Seeker's Trial

Three answers sealed and sent to the GM alone,
will earn you a rank few have known.

  1. Romance once budded here,
    same place we lost many near and dear.

    except behind the High King's door.
  2. Glory be his name,
    Brow of steel and Light aflame.
    Once had a brother, turned to twilight,
    we amongst the Legion had to fight.
  3. Axe bloodied with youth,
    for nothing this monster could soothe.
    Track him down we must,
    should no one else fall to his lust.
